About Mila Café

A little about who we are, where we come from, and what we stand for.

OUR STORY

Born from a love of good coffee and good company.

Mila Café Brews started as a simple idea - a place where the coffee is always fresh, the food is made with care, and every customer feels at home the moment they walk in.

We opened our doors in Johannesburg with one goal: to make your day a little better. Whether you stop by for your morning flat white, a quick lunch, or a relaxed afternoon with a friend, we want every visit to feel unhurried and worth it.

Everything on our menu is prepared fresh. We source our beans from trusted local roasters and keep our food menu focused - a small selection done well.

WHAT WE BELIEVE IN

Simple things done well.

01

Quality over quantity

A focused menu of dishes and drinks we are genuinely proud of. We would rather do fewer things exceptionally than many things averagely.

02

Freshness every time

Nothing sits. Coffee is brewed to order. Food is prepared fresh each day. If it is not at its best, it does not leave the counter.

03

A welcoming space

Mila Café is for everyone - whether you are working alone, catching up with a friend, or grabbing something on the go. All are welcome.

04

Community first

We are a neighbourhood café. We support local suppliers, hire locally, and give back to the community that makes us who we are.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND MILA

Meet the team.

Mila Dlamini

Founder & Head Barista

Mila started the café after ten years in the hospitality industry. Her passion for specialty coffee and her warmth with people are what shaped everything about this place.

Sipho Nkosi

Head Chef

Sipho brings a background in restaurant kitchens and a love of simple, seasonal cooking. He designs a menu that is honest, fresh and satisfying every single day.

Thabo Mokoena

Café Manager

Thabo keeps everything running smoothly - from the morning rush to the quiet afternoon close. His attention to detail means every customer experience is a good one.
